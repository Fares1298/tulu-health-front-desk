Tulu builds that picture automatically.

Operations Director

Your membership business is being run in spreadsheets.

What has each member used? Who renews next month? What did their labs cost? What margin is left on the membership? Most practices piece this together by hand.

Tulu keeps it live in one operating view.

Clinical Staff

Someone is still chasing every missing result.

Ordered. Pending. Received. Filed. Reviewed. Followed up. A surprising amount of clinical staff time disappears into keeping those five words straight.

Tulu tracks the workflow automatically.

Patient

Tulu gives them one view of their results, progress and next steps.
