AI Service · Care Navigator

Grow revenue per patient by guiding them to their right next step.

The problem

In a membership practice, your growth isn't only new patients, it's depth of care per existing one. But the right next panel, protocol, or program goes unoffered because no one can track, patient by patient, who's ready for it.

The fix
