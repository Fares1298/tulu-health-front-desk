Your best revenue is sitting in your lapsed list.

AI Care Navigator

Grow revenue per patient by guiding them to their right next step.

Growth in a membership practice includes depth of care, but the right panel, protocol or program can go unoffered. Biomarkers, wearable signals and protocol cadence identify who is genuinely due; Navigator reaches out with the appropriate next step and escalates to a clinician when the data warrants. Recommendations grounded in real patient data can lift revenue per member while deepening care and the relationship.
