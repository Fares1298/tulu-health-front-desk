AI Service · Care Navigator

Grow revenue per patient by guiding them to their right next step.

The problem

In a membership practice, your growth isn't only new patients, it's depth of care per existing one. But the right next panel, protocol, or program goes unoffered because no one can track, patient by patient, who's ready for it.

The fix

The Twin watches every patient's biomarkers, wearable signals, and protocol cadence, and flags who's genuinely due for what. Navigator reaches out with the right next step, and escalates to a clinician when the data warrants.

Growth that makes the patient more cared for, not less.

Every appropriately-timed next step lifts revenue per member and deepens the relationship at the same time. Because every recommendation is grounded in the patient's real data, it reads as medicine, not marketing.
