FAQ

Questions Hospitals Ask Us First.

What's the difference between Convert IQ and Revive?

Convert IQ handles incoming demand: calls and WhatsApp messages from people who want to book. Revive handles outgoing follow-up: it reaches patients who lapsed, missed an appointment or are overdue for a follow-up, and books them back in. Each can run on its own.

Can we start with just one?

Yes. Most teams start where they lose the most visits, usually after-hours calls or no-shows, and add the second service once the first is live.

Will it work with our phone system, WhatsApp and EHR / EMR?

Your patients keep using the same numbers and WhatsApp account. Most EHR and CRM integrations go live in 1–2 weeks, and we confirm the exact scope for your systems before launch.

Which languages does it speak?

Tell us which languages your patients use and we'll show you the agent speaking them in the demo.

Does the AI give medical advice?

No. It answers administrative questions from content your team has approved. Anything clinical or urgent goes straight to your clinical team under the escalation rules you set.

What happens when a patient wants a person?

They get one. The agent transfers the call or chat to your staff with the full conversation so far, so nobody starts over.

How is patient data protected?

Tulu is SOC-2 and HIPAA compliant. Outreach respects each patient's consent and opt-out preferences.

How do we begin?

Book a demo. Bring your inquiry volume and a sample of your patient list, and we'll show you where Convert IQ and Revive can recover visits.
